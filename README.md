# GET TO THE GIG

A browser game for the BOB tour. Six gigs, six cities, ten days, two vans.

**Karlsruhe** 26.11 · **Frankfurt** 27.11 · **Köln** 28.11 · **Bremen** 03.12 · **Berlin** 04.12 · **Hamburg** 05.12

Five bands, one backline, and a fiftieth birthday somewhere in the middle of it.
Scheisse Minnelli, Special Forces, Party Force, the Leftbehinds, and Raw Power
for the first three nights.

More tunes and info: **scheisseminnelli.bandcamp.com**

## Playing it

Open the page. Tap or click once so the browser lets the sound through.

- **Arrow keys** move
- **SPACE** does the thing — gas, lift, talk, swing, carry
- **ENTER** always means carry on
- **F** or double-click for full screen

On a phone the buttons under the picture do the same job. Turn the phone
sideways if a level feels cramped — there is a lot more room in landscape.

## The six levels

**Karlsruhe — the van run.** Get both vans and everybody in them to the Alte
Hackerei without losing the backline out of the back doors.

**Frankfurt — the door.** Nobody is working it, so you are. Eighteen people, a
sliding scale, and a PA that has to be paid for before anyone else gets anything.

**Köln — the pit.** Catch the stage divers, keep the beer topped up, and do not
put anybody's teeth through the monitor.

**Bremen — the Magazinkeller.** No clock. Three things need sorting before the
doors open, and everybody wants a word.

**Berlin — the load-in.** Five pieces of gear, two flights of stairs, one dog
that will not move.

**Hamburg — the last one.** Find the three band members who wandered off, find
the setlist blowing down the Reeperbahn, and get to the Lobusch.

## The merch code

Finish a tour in the black and the game gives you a code. Screenshot it and show
it at the merch table at any of the six shows for one free surprise item, while
they last.

The tour has to at least break even. Get the bands paid and the flights covered
and there is something in it for you.

`check.html` on this site is the validator for whoever is working the table.

## Credits

Everybody in this game is a real person on the real tour, drawn from photographs
they sent. The full cast rolls at the end of a finished tour.

## Technical

One HTML file, no dependencies, no build step, no tracking, no cookies. Scores
and codes are stored in your own browser and go nowhere else. Clearing your
browser data clears them.

Works offline once loaded. Save the page and it still runs.
